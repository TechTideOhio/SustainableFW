# ForestTwin Product Marketing Context

## Product Overview
* **One-liner**: ForestTwin is the carbon asset intelligence platform that turns satellite imagery and IoT sensor data into living 3D replicas of forest assets.
* **What it does**: Creates interactive 3D digital twins of forests, tracks carbon sequestration in real time, generates audit-ready carbon ledgers compatible with Verra VCS and Gold Standard registries. Combines satellite (Sentinel, Landsat, Planet), IoT sensors, drone surveys, and field assessments.
* **Product category**: Carbon Asset Intelligence / Environmental Data Platform
* **Product type**: B2B SaaS
* **Business model**: Tiered subscription: Monitor ($49/mo, 1 site), Portfolio ($299/mo, 25 sites), Enterprise (custom)
* **URL**: https://digitaltwinfw.netlify.app
* **Parent org**: TechTide AI (https://techtideai.io)
* **Founder**: Alex Cinovoj (https://alexcinovoj.com)

## Target Audience
* **Target companies**: Sustainability-focused enterprises, carbon project developers, forest management companies, ESG reporting teams, carbon credit registries
* **Decision-makers**: Chief Sustainability Officers, Heads of ESG, Carbon Portfolio Managers, Environmental Data Directors
* **Primary use case**: Converting raw environmental data into verifiable, audit-ready carbon credits with full transparency
* **Jobs to be done**: (1) Verify carbon sequestration claims with multi-source data, (2) Generate audit-ready ESG reports, (3) Monitor forest assets in real time

## Problems & Pain Points
* **Core problem**: Carbon credit verification relies on outdated quarterly PDF reports, manual field surveys, and satellite-only estimates with 20-40% error margins
* **Why alternatives fall short**: Satellite-only platforms miss understory biomass; manual audits are slow and expensive; spreadsheets cannot scale across multiple sites
* **What it costs them**: Failed audits, greenwashing accusations, rejected credits, delayed registry submissions, reputational damage
* **Emotional tension**: Fear of regulatory enforcement (EU Green Claims Directive, 400+ actions), anxiety about credit permanence

## Competitive Landscape
* **Direct**: Pachama, NCX, Sylvera. Fall short because they are satellite-only without IoT ground truth integration.
* **Secondary**: Manual forestry consultants. Fall short because they cannot provide real-time monitoring or scale.
* **Indirect**: Spreadsheet/PDF-based internal tracking. Fall short because they are not auditable and data gets stale.

## Differentiation
* **Key differentiators**: Multi-source data fusion (satellite + LiDAR + IoT), living 3D digital twins (not static maps), audit-ready carbon ledger, real-time anomaly detection
* **How we do it differently**: We create a living, interactive 3D replica that updates continuously, not a flat dashboard
* **Why that is better**: Every carbon credit is traceable from sensor to registry; audit time drops from weeks to hours
* **Why customers choose us**: Board-ready visuals that executives understand; eliminates the trust gap in carbon claims
